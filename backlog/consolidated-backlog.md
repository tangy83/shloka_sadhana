## Effort & Cost Estimation

### Total Development Effort
- **P0 (62 items)**: ~480 person-weeks
- **P1 (123 items)**: ~920 person-weeks
- **P2 (66 items)**: ~450 person-weeks
- **TOTAL**: ~1,850 person-weeks (~36 person-years)

### Realistic Timeline
- **With 4-person team**: 9-10 years (too slow)
- **With 6-person team**: 6-7 years (still slow)
- **With 10-person team**: 3.5-4 years (reasonable)
- **With 15-person team**: 2.5 years (optimal if well-coordinated)

### Recommended Approach
**Phased team scaling**:
- **Phase 1 (Months 1-3)**: 4 engineers + 1 designer + 1 PM = 6 people
- **Phase 2 (Months 4-6)**: 5 engineers + 1 designer + 1 PM + 1 growth = 8 people
- **Phase 3 (Months 7-12)**: 6 engineers + 2 designers + 1 PM + 2 specialists = 11 people

**Focus on P0 + P1 first**, evaluate P2 based on:
- User feedback
- Revenue/growth metrics
- Competitive landscape
- Resource availability
