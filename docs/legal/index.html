Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 7, 2026

No tracking · No ads · All data stays on your device

Introduction

Sadhana ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect and store the following information locally on your device only:

  • Practice history and session data
  • Streak and statistics information
  • App preferences and settings
  • Favourite shlokas and bookmarks
  • Notification preferences

We do NOT collect: personal information, email addresses, names, analytics, advertising identifiers, or any data linked to your identity.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Track your spiritual practice progress
  • Send daily reminder notifications (if you enable them)
  • Personalise your experience

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not transmit your personal practice data to any external servers. Your information remains private and under your control. You can delete all app data at any time through the Settings menu.

Location Information

The app may request access to your location to calculate accurate auspicious times (muhurat) based on your geographical coordinates. This calculation happens entirely on your device. Your location is never stored persistently or transmitted to any server.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, we use your device's local notification system to send you practice reminders. No notification data is sent to external servers. You can disable notifications at any time through the app settings or your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share your data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All data is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Data Security

All data stored on your device is protected by your device's built-in security (passcode, Face ID, fingerprint, etc.). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take every reasonable precaution.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data stored in the app
  • Delete all your data through Settings → Clear Data
  • Opt out of notifications at any time
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all local data

Because no data is held on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf beyond what you can already do yourself.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
